  ## Page 1 — A small field guide

  Stupid Simple Blogging · Sample edition

  Three pages for a slower afternoon. No destination required.

  ![An illustrated shoreline: green waves and a sandy coast beneath a warm orange sun.](/media/sample-shore.png)

  Take a short walk. Notice one thing you would usually pass. Bring back a sentence, a shape, or a question.

  Print it, keep it on your phone, or leave it open beside a notebook.

  A little room for noticing.

  ## Page 2 — Leave the route a little open

  Out there.

  Fifteen minutes is enough. So is the view from a window.

  1. **Look for a line.** The edge of a shadow. A branch against the sky. A path that bends out of sight.
  2. **Wait for a change.** Stay with the view for a minute. What moves? What stays? What did you miss at first?
  3. **Keep one detail.** Write a sentence. Draw a shape. Record a few quiet seconds. One thing is enough.

  > There is no right thing to come back with.

  Leave places as you found them. The smallest observation can make a good story.

  ## Page 3 — Keep a little of it

  Back here.

  The printable page leaves blank space for a date, a place, and these four prompts. You can also answer them in your own notebook or text editor:

  - One thing I noticed
  - A colour, sound, or shape
  - The sentence I want to keep
  - Something to return to

  An original sample booklet for Stupid Simple Blogging. Made to be printed, scribbled on, and changed.

  Until the next small detour.
