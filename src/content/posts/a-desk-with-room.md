I cleared the desk until only three things remained: a notebook, a pencil, and a cup. The cup was mostly an excuse to sit down.

![An open notebook, pencil, and coffee on a warm wooden desk.](/media/sample-desk.png)

The empty space did not make the work easier. It made the next thing easier to see.

## One useful question

Before starting, I wrote: **What would make today feel complete?** Not impressive. Complete.

| Keep | Put aside |
| --- | --- |
| One small task | A list of twenty |
| A rough first pass | The perfect opening |
| A ten-minute window | Waiting for a free day |

By lunch there was a paragraph on the page. It was not the paragraph I expected, which was a good sign.
