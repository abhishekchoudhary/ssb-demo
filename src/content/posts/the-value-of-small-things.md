Some of the most useful things I own are also the simplest. A notebook that fits in a pocket. A mug with a comfortable handle. A list of books I might read one day.

Their value is in how often I return to them.

## A small experiment

For a week, I tried writing down one thing each day that I would otherwise forget. Nothing dramatic: the light on a wall, a good sentence, the name of a tree.

By Sunday I had seven small reasons to pay more attention.

The point was not to turn the habit into a system. It was to make a little more of the day visible.
