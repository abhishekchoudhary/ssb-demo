I left the house with a plan to get somewhere. By the water, the plan felt less interesting than the way there.

The tide had drawn a new line across the sand. Someone had left a very small stack of stones. Beyond the bend, the path disappeared into the trees.

## What came back with me

Not much: a sentence, an idea for a drawing, and the sense that the afternoon had become a little larger.
