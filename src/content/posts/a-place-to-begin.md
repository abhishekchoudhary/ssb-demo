There is something useful about a blank page. It asks very little: a thought, a sentence, perhaps an observation from the walk home.

This journal is a place to collect those things. Not everything needs to become a project. Sometimes it is enough to notice, and to write it down.

## Making a little room

The internet can feel like a place where everything has to be finished. A personal site offers a different possibility. You can leave a question open. You can return to an idea months later.

> Start with what you noticed. The rest can follow.

For now, the plan is simple:

- Write when there is something worth remembering.
- Keep the pages comfortable to read.
- Let the collection grow at its own pace.
