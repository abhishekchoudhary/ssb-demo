A small reading-time tool#Link to section:A small reading-time tool

Estimate reading time by dividing the word count by 200 words per minute and rounding up to a whole minute. This is a rough planning aid; people and texts vary.

Words Estimated time
200 1 minute
500 3 minutes
1,000 5 minutes

The optional calculator below applies that same formula. It requires JavaScript; the explanation and examples above do not.

Make this your own#Link to section:Make this your own

Open this page in the editor and choose a column. Write with Markdown, insert media, or add an HTML snippet. Change column widths, move rows, and use Undo if you change your mind.

Static HTML belongs to the reading page. Interactive HTML runs in its own frame, alongside an explanation that is available to everyone.
