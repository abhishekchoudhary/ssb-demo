The long way home

A shoreline, a detour, and six seconds of quiet.

Your nameOctober 16, 2026

I left the house with a plan to get somewhere. By the water, the plan felt less interesting than the way there.

The tide had drawn a new line across the sand. Someone had left a very small stack of stones. Beyond the bend, the path disappeared into the trees.

A walk in three frames#Link to section:A walk in three frames

The film is six seconds long and silent. That is about how long I stood still before deciding to keep walking.

Some days, the detour is the part you came for.

What came back with me#Link to section:What came back with me

Not much: a sentence, an idea for a drawing, and the sense that the afternoon had become a little larger.
